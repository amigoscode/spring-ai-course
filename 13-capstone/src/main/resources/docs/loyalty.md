# Amigos Club loyalty programme

## Joining

The Amigos Club is free. Every customer with an account is a member automatically.

## Earning points

You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend on books. Shipping costs don't earn points.
Points appear in your account when your order is delivered.

## Using points

Every 100 points can be turned into a $5 voucher for your next order. Vouchers are valid
for 6 months. Points expire 2 years after you earned them.

## Birthday gift

Members who have added their birthday to their account get a $5 voucher every year in their
birthday month.
