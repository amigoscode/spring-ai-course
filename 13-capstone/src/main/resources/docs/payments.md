# Payments

## How you can pay

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Amigos Books gift cards.
We don't accept cash, cheques or bank transfers.

Your card is charged when the order leaves the warehouse, not when you place it.

## Gift cards

Gift cards come in $10, $25, $50 and $100. They're valid for 2 years from the day they were
bought and can be used for printed books and e-books. A gift card can't be exchanged for cash.

## Prices and taxes

All prices include VAT for the European Union. Prices are shown in US dollars and converted
by your bank or PayPal at the time of payment.

## Never share card details in the chat

Our assistant will never ask for your card number, CVV or password. If someone asks for them,
it isn't us.
