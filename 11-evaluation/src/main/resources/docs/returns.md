# Returns and refunds

## Returning a printed book

You can return any printed book within 30 days of delivery, for any reason. The book must be
in the condition you received it. Start a return from your account page, print the label and
drop the parcel at any post office.

Returns are free in the European Union. From the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway the
return label costs $6.

## Refunds

We refund the price of the book within 5 business days of receiving it, to the payment method
you used. Shipping costs are refunded only if the book was faulty or we sent the wrong one.

## E-books

E-books can't be returned once you've downloaded them. If an e-book file is broken, contact
support and we'll send you a working copy or a refund.

## Faulty books

If a book has missing or damaged pages, we replace it for free, even after the 30 days,
for up to one year after delivery.
