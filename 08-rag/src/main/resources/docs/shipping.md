# Shipping

## Where we ship

Amigos Books ships printed books to every country in the European Union, the United Kingdom,
Switzerland and Norway. We don't ship outside Europe yet. E-books are available worldwide.

## Delivery times and prices

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.90. Standard delivery is free for
orders over $50.

Express delivery takes 2 business days and costs $9.90, whatever the order total.

Orders placed before 2pm (Lisbon time) on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

## Tracking

You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse. You can also ask
the assistant for the status of an order with its order id, like ORD-1001.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel hasn't arrived 10 business days after it was shipped, or arrives damaged,
contact support@amigosbooks.example with your order id. We send a replacement or refund
you in full.
